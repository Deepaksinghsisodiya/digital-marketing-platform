## 4. Legal Compliance Content

### Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)
- Full disclosure of data handling under Indian **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** and applicable US state privacy standards (CCPA/CPRA).
- Clear definitions of personal data collected, lawful processing purposes, cookie tracking disclosures, and user rights (access, rectification, erasure).
- Grievance Officer and Data Protection Officer contact details.

### Terms of Service (`/terms`)
- Professional service engagement standards, intellectual property ownership (100% transferred to client upon final milestone payment), warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, and jurisdiction clauses.

### Cookie Policy (`/cookies`)
- Categorized inventory of cookies: Essential (session, CSRF, region), Analytical (GA4, PostHog), and Marketing (Meta Pixel, Google Ads).
- Direct link to open the interactive Cookie Preference Manager to revoke consent at any time.

### Accessibility Statement (`/accessibility`)
- Formal declaration of adherence to **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** guidelines.
- Description of accessible keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, high-contrast ratios, and screen-reader optimizations.
- Direct contact mechanism to report any accessibility barrier.
