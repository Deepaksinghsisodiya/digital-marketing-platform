## 1. Editorial Objectives & Audience Profiles

Nexora speaks directly to two primary buyer personas:

### Persona A: The Technical Decision-Maker (CTO, VP of Engineering, Head of Product)
- **Primary Need:** Technical competence, clean architecture, sub-second performance, security compliance, maintainable modern codebases (Next.js, TypeScript, APIs).
- **Messaging Requirement:** Concrete architecture specifications, verified stacks, API contracts, zero superficial marketing jargon.

### Persona B: The Commercial Decision-Maker (CMO, Founder, Managing Director, Growth Lead)
- **Primary Need:** Predictable customer acquisition, CAC reduction, pipeline velocity, verifiable ROI, high-converting funnel design.
- **Messaging Requirement:** Business outcomes, conversion metrics, search dominance, transparent retainers, fast execution velocity.

## 3. Page Content Quality Guardrails

1. **Original Thought Leadership:** No generic AI-generated fluff. All articles and service guides must provide actionable, technical frameworks with real code snippets, tabular comparisons, or tangible methodology diagrams.
2. **Fact-Checked Entity Data:** No hallucinated certifications, fake employee counts, fabricated office locations, or fake partner logos.
3. **Structured Entity Representation:** Every article and service page incorporates semantic entity markup (`Person`, `Organization`, `Service`) to maximize citation frequency in Generative AI engines (SearchGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).
4. **Transparent Proposal Model:** Instead of publishing fabricated fixed prices, explain cost drivers transparently (landing page counts, architectural complexity, integration scope) and provide a low-friction "Request Custom Proposal" funnel.
