## 2. Logo System & Brand Marks

### Requirements & Geometry
- **Brand Symbol:** Minimalist geometric vector mark representing the convergence of Growth (upward angle), Technology (clean architectural bracket), and Intelligence (converging focal point).
- **Wordmark:** Clean uppercase letterforms with balanced optical kerning: `NEXORA`.
- **Approved Variants:**
  1. **Primary Horizontal Logo (Light Backgrounds):** Dark Midnight Navy wordmark (`#101828`) with Royal Blue & Violet convergence symbol.
  2. **Primary Horizontal Logo (Dark Backgrounds):** Pure White wordmark (`#FFFFFF`) with Electric Blue & Light Violet symbol.
  3. **Monochrome Dark:** 100% Solid Midnight Navy (`#101828`) for print or single-color applications.
  4. **Monochrome Light:** 100% Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for dark video overlays or high-contrast footers.
  5. **Favicon & Social Avatar:** Concentrated geometric convergence symbol scaled down to 16x16, 32x32, 180x180 (Apple touch), and 512x512 PNG/SVG.

*Rule:* Never stretch, rotate, apply drop-shadows, or place the logo on busy, low-contrast photographic backgrounds.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

### 1. Primary Heading: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Weights:** 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold).
- **Usage:** Hero titles, section headings (H1, H2, H3), key stat callouts.
- **Styling:** Tight letter-spacing (`tracking-tight`), crisp modern geometric appeal.

### 2. Primary Body & Editorial: Inter
- **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold).
- **Usage:** All paragraph text, bullet points, article prose, form labels.
- **Styling:** Optimal line-height (`leading-relaxed`), exceptional legibility across OLED and LCD displays.

### 3. Supporting UI & Microcopy: DM Sans
- **Weights:** 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold).
- **Usage:** Navigation links, badges, button labels, breadcrumbs, table headers.
- **Styling:** Clean geometry, clear legibility at small sizes (11px–14px).

## 5. Visual Asset Guidelines (No Stock Photos)

- **Abstract Graphic Compositions:** Replace generic stock photography with custom abstract SVG illustrations featuring data flows, node connectors, code structures, and organic growth curves.
- **Iconography:** Standardize on **Lucide React**. Maintain a consistent `strokeWidth={1.75}` and standard bounding box dimensions (`w-5 h-5` for inline, `w-6 h-6` for cards, `w-8 h-8` for hero highlights).
- **Elevation & Shadows:** Use soft, diffused shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-xl`) paired with 1px border lines (`border border-slate-200/80`). Avoid harsh, dark dropshadows.
