## 2. Strategic Insights & Architectural Synthesis

### What Nexora Must Adopt Across All Pages
1. **The 3-Pillar Unified Architecture:** Digital Growth, Technology Solutions, and AI & Automation must be explicitly represented in the header navigation, hero section, capabilities showcase, and footer.
2. **20-Point Service Landing Page Standard:** Every service page must include SEO metadata, breadcrumbs, hero, business problems, our solutions, tangible deliverables, 4-step process, tech stack, industry applications, FAQs, and dual CTAs.
3. **Dual Market Localization (India & USA):** Transparent multi-country support with localized currency formatting (INR `₹` for India, USD `$` for US), time-zone scheduling, and compliant legal disclosures.
4. **Accessible Component Primitives:** Built on Radix UI and Tailwind CSS with full keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and screen-reader ARIA live regions.
5. **Direct Backend CRM Integration:** Seamless lead routing to ASP.NET Core API (`/api/v1/public/leads`) preserving UTM campaign parameters, referrer, and lead scoring.

### What Nexora Must Strictly Avoid
1. **No Stock Photography of Random Models:** Use authentic abstract SVG compositions, data flow diagrams, and real technology UI artifacts.
2. **No Fabricated Claims or False Accreditations:** Never invent Google Premier Partner badges, fake client testimonials, artificial 5-star review counts, or fictional US office addresses.
3. **No Heavy Autoplay Video Heroes:** Keep page performance blazing fast (LCP <= 2.5s, First Load JS <= 150kB).
4. **No Scroll-Hijacking or Disruptive Modals:** Ensure smooth native scrolling with complete support for keyboard navigation and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
