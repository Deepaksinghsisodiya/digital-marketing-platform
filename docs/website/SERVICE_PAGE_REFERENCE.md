## 1. Executive Standard: The 20-Point Service Architecture

Generic agency websites fail because their service pages are shallow (300 words of generic marketing buzzwords). Nexora mandates a **20-Point Comprehensive Service Architecture** for every published service page across Digital Growth, Technology Solutions, and AI & Automation.

Every service landing page must contain:

1. **SEO Title:** Formula: `[Specific Service Name] — Nexora | [Core Value Proposition]` (< 60 chars).
2. **Meta Description:** High-intent commercial summary addressing target search queries and key deliverables (< 160 chars).
3. **Canonical URL & Slug:** Clean semantic path (e.g., `/services/seo`, `/services/web-development`).
4. **Structured Breadcrumb:** Visual and JSON-LD schema breadcrumb navigation (`Home > Services > [Service Name]`).
5. **Hero Eyebrow:** Strategic category badge (e.g., `PILLAR 1: DIGITAL GROWTH`).
6. **Hero Heading:** High-impact commercial value proposition focusing on business outcomes.
7. **Hero Description:** Clear, articulate summary of scope, execution capabilities, and target clients.
8. **Primary CTA:** High-intent direct action (e.g., *"Schedule Strategy Session"*, *"Request Architecture Audit"*).
9. **Secondary CTA:** Exploratory action (e.g., *"View Technical Deliverables"*, *"Explore Process"*).
10. **Service Introduction & Strategic Scope:** 250–350 word authoritative overview of why this discipline matters in 2026.
11. **Core Business Problems We Solve:** 4–6 specific client pain points with impact quantification (e.g., wasted ad spend, slow page loads, poor lead quality).
12. **Our Technical Solutions & Approach:** Detailed breakdown of how Nexora resolves each problem.
13. **Tangible Artifact Deliverables:** 6 concrete, physical/digital assets the client receives (not vague ideas, but tangible deliverables like audit matrices, Figma design tokens, CI/CD pipelines).
14. **Working Methodology (The 4-Step Process):** Tailored implementation phases (*Discover, Strategize, Execute, Optimize*).
15. **Tools, Platforms & Technologies:** Verified software, platforms, APIs, and frameworks used in delivery.
16. **Industry Vertical Applications:** How this service adapts to SaaS, E-commerce, Healthcare, Real Estate, and Professional Services.
17. **Demonstration Project / Case Study Highlight:** Real architecture breakdown or verified demonstration case.
18. **Structured Service FAQs:** 5–8 targeted commercial and technical questions with `FAQPage` schema.
19. **Cross-Pillar Related Services:** 3 interconnected services linking Digital Growth, Technology, and AI.
20. **Final Conversion CTA Block:** High-contrast closing offer with direct consultation booking and SLA commitment.
